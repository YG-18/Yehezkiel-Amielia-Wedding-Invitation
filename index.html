Our Love Story

They say first impressions matter... ours certainly did—but not in the way you'd expect. Our story began back in junior high school, where she somehow managed to dislike me before she even really knew me. Funny enough, a grade one farewell trip turned strangers into friends, and not long after, friends into sweethearts.

Then one day in our teenagers days, she looked at me seriously and said, "If we're doing this, I want a love that leads to marriage." Those words caught me by surprise, but they also became a promise I carried in my heart.

The years passed with lectures, careers, prayers, and countless ordinary days that quietly built an extraordinary love. With God's grace guiding every step, comes the day where I knelt down and asked the easiest yet most important question of my life and She said "Yes."

Now, our long voyage has found its shore. Our fourteen-year journey has led us to this sacred moment. Before God's altar, we will joined together in Christ love and make our covenant to acknowledge Christ as the Head of our marriage, that every joy, every trial, and every season of our lives may always begin and end in Him.

P.S. So I guess "never judge someone before you truly know them... cause you might ended up legally taking their last name." 😉
